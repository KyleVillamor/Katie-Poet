The Little Things

A flower by the window, a breeze across the trees, The sunlight finding silver in the dancing of the leaves. A favourite song, a funny thought, a reason just to smile, The little things can turn a day to something sweet awhile. Like sampaguita in the evening, blooming soft and white, Or hibiscus in the morning, full of colour, full of light, There is a kind of magic in the ordinary view— The world is full of wonders in the things we wander through. So may your days hold moments that are lovely, bright, and small, And may you always find some joy in the middle of it all.
for the small things that make us smile ✧

May You Bloom Brightly

May mornings greet you gently with a sky of golden blue, And may the day have something unexpectedly good for you. May laughter find you often, may your worries learn to rest, And may you see that trying hard is sometimes doing your best. Like hibiscus turning sunward in the warmth of early spring, May you grow in your own season and enjoy the little things. Like sampaguita after rain, serene beneath the sky, May you find your quiet courage when the busy hours fly. And on the days that feel too long, when everything seems slow, Remember even gardens take their own sweet time to grow. There is no need to rush the dawn or race the falling rain; Tomorrow brings another chance to start with hope again.
a little wish for brighter days ✿

A Quiet Goodnight

The day is gently fading, and the stars begin to shine; I hope the night brings peaceful thoughts and gives you quiet time. If anything felt heavy, may it soften with the breeze, And may you find some comfort in the small and simple things. May dreams be soft as petals, may the moon be warm and bright, May sampaguita's sweetness fill the stillness of the night. Tomorrow holds a thousand chances for a brand-new start, For laughter, rest, and little joys that settle in your heart. So goodnight, Katie, sleep well and let the day be done; I hope tomorrow treats you kindly with the rising sun.
rest well, Katie ☾
